A Parasolid-style B-rep kernel, written from scratch, with its CAD workspace

TL;DR: GeometryProcessing is an exact B-rep modeller in C++: topology, intersections, Booleans, tessellation, rolling-ball blends, face operations, sketches and a persistently named feature tree. Every claim on this page comes from a validation program in the repository. SolidArcWorkspace puts the kernel behind a Vulkan + Slang + ImGui workspace that uses the Plasticity keymap. Cliff overflow (BlendValidation Overflow family · PatternStress O1 / O2)

When the blend's foot on a support lands at or past the far end of the edge it trims, the blend is wider than that face (Parasolid: cliff overflow). If the support is a strip of 4 edges and its far edge is convex, the support is consumed: the face across the far edge (the cliff) trims the blend with an exact section, and the blend's rail on the consumed face is dropped. Fillets and chamfers, on straight and circular edges.

🐞 Defects found and fixed

  • Newton drift: a converged point was stepped again at a tangent contact (1.6e-16 → 7.1e-8). The residual test now runs first.
  • Tangent-crossing joints were only √ε accurate and made false pole rows; smooth joints now use transversal operations only.
  • Periodic fold on short seam arcs: the projection hint folds at the middle of the gap.
  • Crash: an elliptical spine reached std::get<CircularArc>. Lie: overlapping blends built a self-intersecting body; DetectSelfClash now gates every blend.

📝 Tolerances (never loosened)

  • Session precision 1e-8 · predicates exact (Shewchuk)
  • Closed-form gate 1e-9 · max(1, V*) · join 1e-6 · audit edge 1e-5
  • Closure gap 1e-9 · tangent-chain G1 1e-9 rad
  • Tolerance order: vertex ≥ edge ≥ face (Jackson 1995)
