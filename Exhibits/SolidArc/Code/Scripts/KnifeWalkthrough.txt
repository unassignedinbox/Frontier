# SolidArc knife walkthrough (Shift+K, Blender KitOps style) — a shape drawn on a face is filled and cut into the body to
# a typed depth or straight through; a library insert (boolean library) follows the faces, is placed, sized and cut,
# added as a boss (Q), or left as a body of its own (R); picked bodies cut each other with the cutters kept (K).
# Run: SolidArcCapture --specimen --reset-files --recovery …/KnifeRecovery.solidarc --document …/Knife.solidarc
#      --inserts …/KnifeInserts --script Scripts/KnifeWalkthrough.txt
# Closed forms (2 m cube, z 0 … 2): circle r 0.3 cut 0.5 deep = 0.045π · rectangle 0.4 × 0.4 through 2 m = 0.32 ·
# square pocket insert of width 0.4 (depth 0.3 widths) = 0.4² × 0.12 = 0.0192 · the same insert raised (Q) adds 0.0192 ·
# a separate insert body (0.35 widths tall) = 0.4² × 0.14 = 0.0224.
