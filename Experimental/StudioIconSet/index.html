DESIGN RULES

  • 24 × 24 grid, 2 px safe margin (dashed box in the preview).
  • 1.5 px strokes, round caps and joins, no fills except small dots and tinted areas.
  • Two layers: neutral shape + one family colour on the part that carries the meaning. Mono mode drops the colour and still reads.
  • One colour per studio: amber basics, blue UV, orange particles, green forces, violet launchers.
  • Flat only: no glow, gradients or shadows.
