# DraftingWorkspace object-snapping walkthrough — the Plasticity point picker while drawing and moving: endpoint,
# midpoint, centre, tangent and perpendicular snaps land on the exact carriers; n / b / t / x / y / z lock a direction
# through the last point; s squares a rectangle; G moves a grab-point snap onto a target snap; x-ray reaches hidden points.
# Run: DraftingWorkspaceCapture --specimen --reset-files --recovery …/SnapRecovery.drafting.toml
#      --document …/Snap.drafting.toml --script Experimental/DraftingWorkspace/Scripts/SnappingWalkthrough.txt
# Bracket specimen: block 4 × 2.5 × 1 with its corner at the origin, boss, hole, Fillet1, Chamfer1 — volume 10.22278.
# Closed forms: circle C = (−2, 1.2), r 0.6; from P = (0, 0) the tangent point is C + r(cos α û + sin α ẑ × û) with
# cos α = r / |P − C|: T = (-1.569338679, 1.617768868); the perpendicular foot from (2, 0) is C + r (M − C) / |M − C|
# = (-1.425304229, 1.027591269).

# ① Shift+W centre circle r 0.6 at (−2, 1.2) on the grid
frames 3
key W Shift
click world -2.0 1.2 0.0
frames 2
click world -1.4 1.2 0.0
frames 2
expect steps == 9

expect snap-z == 0
