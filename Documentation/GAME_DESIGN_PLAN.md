# Frontier — Teleport Gauntlet Racing Game Design Plan

> **Core Identity:** No police. No tracks. No circuits. No laps. Players are teleported straight from one hostile, high-chaos physics gauntlet into the next — keeping their momentum, damage, flames, and latched creatures straight through every portal.
>
> **Design Rule:** Every level must have **violence, panic, scale, and something actively trying to grab, crush, burn, eat, or trap your vehicle every few seconds** while you stay on the gas.

---

## 1. Core Level Roster (User's Original 6)

1. **Conveyor Belt (`foundry` — Implemented)**
   - Massive multi-lane industrial conveyor gauntlet with counter-moving belts, turbo strips, moving steel cargo, stamping presses, side-pushers, rotary sweepers, and giant articulated electromagnet robots pulling both cargo and your car.
2. **Dead Forest**
   - Twisted dead woodland where massive gnarled roots violently erupt straight out of the earth in front of your bumper, whipping across lanes and blocking escape routes.
3. **Overgrown Lab**
   - Ruined botanical/industrial facility overrun by colossal carnivorous plants actively lunging out to snap up, trap, and *eat* vehicles whole.
4. **Demolition Run (`demolish` — Implemented)**
   - Procedurally generated & procedurally fractured urban demolition neighborhood (randomized every entry): players choose crevices, blast straight through drive-through garages, glass storefronts, interior partitions, and brick walls while giant wrecking-ball cranes and C4 demolition blasts bring buildings down around them.
5. **D-Day (`dday` — Implemented)**
   - Omaha Beach gauntlet: players race to breach The Wall while diving aircraft drop bombs, shore turrets track and shell the vehicle, and tanks/trenches provide temporary cover.
6. **Nuclear Wasteland City + Alien Plants**
   - Irradiated ruined metropolis overrun by hostile alien flora, toxic spores, acid rain squalls, and parasitic creatures latching onto vehicles and bleeding fuel dry.

## 2. Approved High-Chaos Teleport Levels

1. **The Capsizing Mega-Ship (Sliding Container Avalanche)**
   - Teleport onto the massive open deck of a container ship actively rolling sideways into the ocean (`10° → 30°` live deck tilt).
   - 40-foot shipping containers snap their chains and slide across the deck like steel avalanches. Weave between sliding 20-ton metal walls, blast through open container tunnels and closing bulkhead doors, and launch off the rising bow ramp into the exit portal before the ship capsizes.
2. **The Derailing Freight Yard (Live 80-Car Train Wreck)**
   - Race across a multi-track rail yard while full-speed runaway freight trains actively derail straight across your path.
   - Boxcars jackknife sideways into steel walls, fuel tankers chain-detonate into rolling fireballs, and tilted flatcars become sudden jump ramps — smash through wooden boxcar walls or vault burning locomotives.
3. **The Live Scrapyard Shredder (Claw Cranes & Crusher Trench)**
   - Dumped inside an automated car-breaking yard that classifies your car as scrap.
   - Giant hydraulic claw arms lunge down to grab your roof and drop you into a spinning metal shredder trench; overhead crushers slam across narrow lanes; smash through stacked junker cars and duck under corrugated steel sheds where the claws cannot reach.
4. **The Failing Hydro-Dam Spillway (Wall of Water Chasing Your Bumper)**
   - A colossal concrete dam ruptures behind you at spawn. A 20-meter wall of churning floodwater, snapped steel turbines, and tumbling concrete surges down the valley floor faster than a hesitant driver.
   - Zero slowing down — clip a pillar and the wave catches your rear bumper and tumbles the car end-over-end. Smash through turbine control booths and jump snapped bridge spans staying ahead of the surge.
5. **The Hollowed-Out Insect Highway (Predatory Swarm Biome)**
   - Broken subterranean highway infested with giant burrowing insects.
   - Drill-worms erupt vertically through asphalt like living mines, launching slabs of road skyward; packs of fuel-mosquitoes dive-bomb your hood; amber-spitters fire hardening glue across exits forcing you to smash through brittle honeycomb side tunnels.
6. **The Rogue Weapons Proving Range**
   - Automated military testing canyon locked onto your vehicle as a live target dummy: rail-mounted sled guns race alongside leading your speed, pneumatic steel flipper-plates snap out of the floor to launch cars sky-high, and parachuting concrete tank-traps rain down from cargo drops shifting safe lanes every second.
7. **The Logging Mountain Slide (Downhill Chaos)**
   - Steep downhill mud-and-timber run while a logging mountain collapses around you: 10-ton stripped tree trunks bounce end-over-end down the slope right over your hood, runaway logging trailers jackknife through wooden cabins, and players smash straight through plank sawmills to escape the main log chute.
8. **The Active Blast Quarry ("Cascading Dynamite Terraces")**
   - Race across the terraced benches of a massive canyon mine while **sequential demolition charges blow entire cliff faces off** right beside your tyres.
   - Thundering boulder avalanches bounce down across lower ledges; unmanned 300-ton haul-truck husks slide sideways across switchbacks; rockslides erase the road behind you.
   - *Hardware Payoff:* **Acoustic Resonance Horn** shatters falling boulders & cracked tunnel plugs into harmless gravel before impact; **Downforce Suction Fans** let you climb **straight UP the steep 50° quarry cliff faces** to skip entire exploding switchbacks; **Winch/Towbar** rips collapsed mine carts out of shortcut tunnels.
9. **The Orbital Launch Trench ("Rocket Ignition Countdown")**
   - Teleport straight into the colossal concrete flame-trench directly beneath a 120-meter heavy-lift rocket at **T-minus 45 seconds**.
   - Retracting service gantries slam across the causeway, ruptured liquid-oxygen pipes flood sections with zero-grip cryo mist, acoustic shockwaves peel concrete off the trench walls, and at **T-minus 10 seconds a roaring wall of white-hot rocket exhaust fire surges down the trench straight behind your rear bumper**.
   - *Hardware Payoff:* **Vehicle Hacking** forces blast gates open at 200 km/h, **Cyclone Turbocharger** drinks the superheated shock-air for blistering escape boost, and you launch off the curved flame-deflector ramp straight into the exit portal as the rocket lifts off.

## 3. New Level Additions (City, Giant Shifting Concrete Maze & Acid Rain)

### A. High-Chaos City Level — **"Sinkhole Downtown (The Tilting Skyline)"**
*A city level built around panic, collapsing infrastructure, and vertical route choices — zero police, zero circuits.*
- **What's actively trying to kill you:** The entire downtown grid is collapsing into a chain of **live subterranean sinkholes and ruptured utility mains**.
  - Entire asphalt intersection slabs **crack and drop 15 meters downward** seconds after your tyres cross them (forcing constant commitment).
  - **High-pressure water & steam mains** blow manhole covers sky-high and erupt into towering geysers that can flip a car onto two wheels.
  - **Skyscraper glass curtain-walls & billboards** shear off leaning towers above, shattering across avenues while **automated rooftop HVAC units and elevators** crash into streets.
  - **Natural Multi-Level Route Choices:** Drop into a flooded subway concourse and smash through ticket turnstiles, blast up a multi-story parking garage spiral and jump through the 3rd-floor parapet onto an elevated monorail deck, or cut straight through ground-floor glass bank lobbies to bypass a freshly opened sinkhole crater.

### B. Giant Shifting Concrete Maze with Hidden Traps — **"The Piston Labyrinth"**
*Colossal, oppressive brutalist concrete corridors (`18–25 m` tall monolith walls) that procedurally randomize, physically shift, crush, and spring concealed industrial traps every run.*
- **Core Shifting Architecture:**
  - **Sliding Hydraulic Megawalls:** Enormous 20-meter-tall concrete monoliths grind sideways on recessed steel floor rails with deafening warning horns, **sealing corridors shut** or **trying to pancake slow cars** between closing faces.
  - **Breakable Cheat Partitions:** Thin weathered concrete/masonry maintenance doors hidden inside corners let observant drivers **smash shortcuts straight across maze rings** instead of taking the long outer corridor.
  - **Overhead Stalker Bridges & Sweeper Blades:** Elevated steel catwalks carry roving floodlights and swinging concrete pendulum counterweights through long straightaways, forcing aggressive weaving.
  - **Vertical Sightlines & Exit Beacon:** Narrow sky shafts let beams of sun/acid rain stab down into the canyon (critical for **Deployable Solar Panel** emergencies!), while a tall pulsing green/amber extraction beacon peeks over the shifting walls so players navigate by sight and instinct rather than a minimap.
- **7 Hidden Brutalist Traps (Camouflaged Until Triggered — Readable by Subtle Tells & Hardware):**
  1. **False-Slab Pitfalls (Counterweighted Trap Floors):** Flush concrete floor panels with hairline expansion seams that look solid until your front tyres roll across — hydraulic latches snap open and the entire 12 m slab tilts vertically into an abyss. *Tell/Counterplay:* Hairline dust puffs & floor rivets visible in **Long-Throw High Beams**; gun it to jump the lip, slam **Air-Brake Spoiler** to stop short, ride the vertical sidewall with **Downforce Fans**, or **Hack** the latches shut ahead of you.
  2. **Pneumatic Slam-Plates (Spring-Loaded Floor Kickers):** Concealed steel-backed floor slabs that violently hinge upward at 45° mid-pass, trying to slam your car into overhead concrete beams or flip you backward. *Tell/Counterplay:* Spot hydraulic oil seams — **EMP Lights / Vehicle Hacking** locks the plate flat, OR ride it deliberately as a launch ramp onto an upper catwalk shortcut!
  3. **Snap-Ambush Alcoves (Concealed Crusher Walls):** Seamless recessed monolith panels flush with blind corridor walls that explode sideways on proximity triggers to T-bone or sandwich your chassis. *Tell/Counterplay:* **Acoustic Resonance Horn** triggers/jams the seam early, **Vehicle Hacking** reverses the piston back into the wall, or fire **Cyclone Afterburner** to shoot the closing gap.
  4. **Drop-Gate Guillotine Shutters (Hidden Ceiling Slabs):** Heavy concrete slabs hidden in dark overhead shadow bays that free-fall across the corridor when your nose breaks an infrared trip-beam. *Tell/Counterplay:* **EMP Headlights / Hacking** kills the trip-beam so the gate stays up; **Ablative Roof Shutters** survive a clip; or spot the sensor housing with **High Beams** and smash the adjacent cheat-partition instead.
  5. **Concealed Reverse-Roller Strips (Hidden Floor Drag Traps):** Flush ribbed concrete tiles inside tight turns that retract covers to expose high-speed reverse traction rollers pulling your tyres backward straight into a closing monolith wall. *Tell/Counterplay:* **Grip Claws** or **Downforce Fans** bite through, **Vehicle Hacking** flips the rollers forward into a turbo sling, or **Front Winch** hooks a corner pylon to haul you clear.
  6. **Corner Ambush Vent Slits (Hidden Acid-Mist / Steam Blasters):** Narrow shadow slits tucked at blind 90° turns blast blinding pressurized steam or corrosive Acid Mist across your windshield right as you brake — slashing tyre grip (`μ`) to zero. *Tell/Counterplay:* **Cyclone Vortex Turbine** inhales the trap blast straight into your turbocharger for an exit surge; **Vortex-Deflector Canards** keep vision clear; **Hydro cars** drink the mist for free power.
  7. **Mirage Dead-End Walls vs. Pincer Alleys:** Some "solid" concrete dead-ends are thin hollow plaster-concrete shells hiding massive shortcuts, while some inviting side alleys are pincer traps whose rear monolith slams shut once you enter. *Tell/Counterplay:* **Acoustic Resonance Horn** shatters mirage walls on approach (solid concrete rings dull); **Long-Throw High Beams** reveal fracture hairlines on fake walls and recessed floor rails on pincer traps.

### C. Global / Biome Hazard — **Acid Rain**
- **Direct Vehicle Physics Effect:**
  - Coat the road in corrosive slick runoff that **drops tyre friction coefficient (`μ`) dramatically** — longer braking distances, wild cornering drift, and easy wheelspin on throttle.
  - Slowly **corrodes exposed tyre rubber** (accelerating tread wear & puncture risk) and mists/etches the windshield in Cockpit/Glass HUD view while you stay under open sky.
- **Tactical Risk-Reward & Shelter Play:**
  - Ducking under **overpasses, drive-through garages, concrete maze arches, subway tunnels, or crane sheds** temporarily shields your car from acid exposure and restores dry patches of traction.
  - **Brilliant Parasite Synergy:** Acid rain **burns and dissolves latched ticks, mosquitoes, vines, and spore-crusts right off your hull**! Covered in heavy weight-ticks inside a tunnel? Deliberately swerve out into the open acid squall for 3 seconds to melt them off, then dive back under cover before your tyres shred.

## 4. Parasites, Creatures & Viruses (Violent, Physical, Skill-Removable)

> **Rule:** Never make a creature just a passive "slower/health bar" debuff. Every creature physically mutates how the car drives AND has a visceral, skill-based driving maneuver to rip, burn, or weaponize it.

### Core Parasites & Counterplay
1. **Fuel-Sucking Mosquitoes (Weaponizable):**
   - Dive-bomb and clamp onto hood/fuel lines, visibly swelling bright orange as they siphon fuel.
   - **Removal / Payoff:** Violent drift-whip (`Space + steer`) flings them off; **slamming a fuel-bloated mosquito into a wall, root barrier, or giant plant detonates it on impact**, blowing open blocked routes or staggering a vehicle-eating plant.
2. **Corner-Latching Ballast Ticks (Asymmetric Weight):**
   - Clamp onto a *specific* corner (front-left fender, rear-right quarter), visibly compressing that suspension strut and pulling steering/braking to that side.
   - **Removal:** Deliberately **grind that exact flank against a concrete wall, house corner, or steel rail at high speed** to shear them off in a shower of sparks — or dive through fire / acid rain to roast/melt them off.
3. **Unhinged Viruses (Chaos Modifiers, Not Just "Slow"):**
   - **Redline Virus:** Jams throttle at 100% + boosts engine power (`+30%`) while cutting brakes for 5 seconds — pure white-knuckle steering survival.
   - **Heavy Armor-Tick Swarm:** Kills agility and adds massive weight, *but* turns the car into a 3-ton battering ram that smashes straight through thick brick/concrete walls that would normally stop you.
   - **Misfire / Backfire Purge:** Burn a small shot of fuel on demand to fire a **rear exhaust flame blast** — roasts chasing mosquitoes or grabbing roots on your rear bumper and gives an emergency shove forward.

### Additional Original Creature Mechanics
4. **Harpoon-Tongue Anchor Grubs (Bumper Snatchers — Overgrown Lab / Dead Forest / Wasteland):**
   - Burrowed ambushers beside alleys and rubble piles shoot an elastic tendril that hooks onto your **rear bumper or axle**, visibly stretching taut and trying to reel you backward into a pit or carnivorous plant maw.
   - **Counterplay:** Floor the throttle + swing sideways to snap the tendon against a sharp corner/pillar (releasing a huge elastic slingshot boost forward!), or fire your **Rear Backfire Purge** / **Sawblade Bumper** to sever it.
5. **Wheel-Well Leech Slugs (Single-Wheel Lock / Asymmetric Drag):**
   - Slither out of wet rubble/sewers and wrap directly around **one wheel hub/brake rotor**, seizing or dragging that single tyre so the car violently pulls into a spin.
   - **Counterplay:** Tap throttle + brake together (burnout/rotor heat) to glow the brake disc red-hot and cook the slug straight out of the wheel arch, or smash through a hard kerb/rubble mound to knock it loose.
6. **Chitin Ram-Beetles (Living Battering Rams — Insect Highway / Maze / Wasteland):**
   - Car-sized armored ambush beetles that charge sideways out of side crevices to T-bone you off course or shove you into closing maze walls.
   - **Counterplay:** Bait them into charging straight into a breakable brick wall, concrete partition, or rival carnivorous plant — they smash open shortcuts *for* you.
7. **Siphon-Barnacle Spores (Windshield & Radiator Chokers):**
   - Explosive spore pods splatter thick translucent goo across your hood, radiator grille (rising engine temp), and windshield.
   - **Counterplay:** Smash straight through hanging barriers, wooden fences, glass storefronts, or drive through acid rain / water geysers to wipe your windshield & radiator clean.
8. **Aggressive Fuel Recovery (Always Forward, Never Stalled):**
   - Recover fuel at 140 km/h by smashing through red fuel drums, ruptured tanker trailers, glowing alien sap pods, or **crushing bloated fuel-ticks under your tyres** to splash fuel directly back into the tank.

## 5. Tool-Based Pickups (Physical Vehicle Survival Tools — NOT Mario-Kart Weapons)

*No homing rockets or generic "destroy other cars" powerups. Pickups are **tactile, mechanical survival tools** bolted onto your chassis with limited charges or short bursts that physically interact with hazards, weather, walls, and creatures:*

1. **Pneumatic Roof/Hull Shock-Purger ("Pulse Jack")**
   - Fires a single violent pneumatic chassis jolt + electro-arc across your fenders: **instantly blasts every latched tick, mosquito, vine, and anchor tendril off your car** AND hops the chassis 0.6 m vertically (enough to clear an erupting root, low rubble lip, or closing floor crack).
2. **Front Hydraulic Ram-Wedge ("Breach Plow")**
   - Temporarily bolts a heavy angled steel demolition plow onto your front bumper (3 impacts or 12 seconds): lets you **cleave straight through reinforced concrete walls, stacked junker cars, thick root walls, and giant plant jaws** with almost zero speed loss.
3. **Studded Spike-Deploy Tyres ("Grip Claws")**
   - Extends retractable tungsten carbide studs out of all 4 tyres for 10 seconds: **completely neutralizes Acid Rain slickness, oil, mud, and tilted ship decks**, letting you carve impossible corners and climb counter-moving conveyor belts at full grip.
4. **Siphon-Harvester Scoop ("Fuel Skimmer")**
   - Magnetized intake scoop under the nose for 12 seconds: automatically vacuums fuel vapor from nearby drums, burning wreckage, and crushed fuel-mosquitoes/ticks without needing a direct dead-center hit, rapidly refilling your tank on the move.
5. **Ablative Roof Shutters (Thermal Roof Shield / Armored Canopy)**
   - Drops armored louvered shutters over the roof and windshield for 10 seconds: **blocks Acid Rain corrosion, falling masonry/glass shards from skyscrapers, aerial shrapnel, and overhead scrapyard crane claws** trying to grab your roof.
6. **Emergency Rear Anchor-Thruster ("Extraction Kick")**
   - Single-use solid-fuel booster cartridge strapped to the rear frame: fires a violent 1.8-second straight-line thrust + scorching rear flame cone — specifically designed to **rip your car free from a giant plant's jaws, outrun a closing concrete maze wall, clear a collapsing sinkhole gap, or roast everything latched to your tail**.
7. **Magnetic Corner-Winch (Environmental Grapple)**
   - Automatically snaps a steel cable onto overhead girders, crane hooks, or concrete maze corner posts on hard turns — letting you **whip-pivot a 90° concrete maze corner at 120 km/h** without scrubbing speed or haul yourself up a tilting ship deck.

## 6. Vehicle Abilities, Power Sources & Functional Modular Hardware

> **Design Rule:** Just like Tool Pickups, Vehicle Abilities and Equipable Parts are **NOT** anti-vehicle PvP weapons and **NOT** hollow cosmetic skins. Every ability, headlight, spoiler, canard, power source, and recovery rig is **real physical or electronic survival hardware** wired directly into driving physics, world infrastructure, weather, and creatures.

---

### A. Core Active Vehicle Abilities (Approved High-Impact Systems)

1. **Acoustic Resonance Horn ("Shock-Wave Grille")**
   - A massive low-frequency industrial air-cannon recessed behind the front grille fires a focused directional pressure wave 15 m ahead.
   - **Gauntlet Payoff:** **Pre-shatters fractured brick, glass, and concrete maze cheat-walls** before impact so you blast through the cloud at full speed; **staggers lunging giant carnivorous plants mid-bite**; blows diving mosquito swarms & toxic spore clouds clear; and deflects falling skyscraper glass off your hood.

2. **Cyclone Vortex Turbine ("Debris-Eater Intake / Turbocharger & Air Vent")**
   - A massive nose-mounted jet turbine / forced-induction air intake actively **inhales surrounding air & debris** for `2 s` and routes it dynamically:
     - **Turbocharger / Air-Vent Mode:** Feeds compressed intake airflow straight into the turbocharger & cooling vents for a massive engine boost + active radiator cooling (clearing heat buildup and Siphon-Barnacle Spore chokes).
     - **Shrapnel Hurricane Blast (Forward):** Sucks in Acid Rain mist, toxic spores, falling skyscraper glass, brick shards, and diving fuel-mosquito swarms, then blasts the superheated mess forward to shred barricades and shove giant plants backward.
     - **Afterburner Exhaust (Rear):** Vents compressed airflow straight out the rear diffuser as a raw jet shove to beat closing maze walls or clear sinkhole jumps.

3. **Tactical Headlight Systems (Equipable / Vehicle-Specific Lights)**
   - Headlights are active survival tools, not just visuals — players equip or swap light rigs depending on chassis & biome:
     - **Scorch-Beam / UV-Thermal Lights:** High-intensity burning beam that **literally sears, blinds, and blisters creatures** caught in the light cone — roasting diving mosquitoes, shrinking back carnivorous plant tendrils, and peeling shadow-lurking swarms off road lanes.
     - **Long-Throw High Beams:** Ultra-penetration spotlights that pierce dense Acid Rain sheets, Nuclear Wasteland dust storms, dark Piston Labyrinth concrete canyons, and Overgrown Lab fog so you read collapsing sinkholes, cheat-walls, and erupting roots far ahead.
     - **Electromagnetic / EMP Headlights *(native to Electric / High-Tech cars, or equipable)*:** Projects a focused electromagnetic beam forward that **fries automated turrets (D-Day / Proving Range), jams Scrapyard claw cranes mid-grab, short-circuits closing hydraulic maze monoliths, and scrambles rogue mechanical hazards** directly in your headlight arc.

4. **Vehicle Hacking System ("Onboard Signal Hijacker")**
   - Lets the driver actively **hack physical world infrastructure at speed** (especially lethal on Electric/Tech chassis or with a Hacking ECU equipped):
     - **Doors, Bulkheads & Maze Gates:** Force closing hydraulic Piston Labyrinth monoliths to stall/re-open, crack locked Overgrown Lab blast doors, or pop Mega-Ship bulkhead shortcuts straight ahead of your bumper.
     - **Streetlights & City Infrastructure (Sinkhole Downtown / Demolition Run):** Overload streetlamp transformers to arc-shock nearby swarms, drop security bollards blocking shortcuts, and flip automated traffic barriers.
     - **Industrial Machinery (Conveyor Belt / Scrapyard / Proving Range):** Reverse a counter-moving conveyor belt into a turbo runway, hijack a Scrapyard crane claw to drop wreckage away from your lane, or trigger Proving Range flipper-plates as instant launch ramps.

5. **Underbody Downforce Fans ("Wall-Climbing Suction Fans")**
   - High-RPM chassis fans blast air downward / pull intense negative-pressure suction under the floorpan, generating real mechanical downforce independent of forward speed.
   - **Gauntlet Payoff:** **Defies Acid Rain ice-slickness on demand** and lets players **climb absurdly steep walls, tilted `30°+` Capsizing Mega-Ship decks, collapsed city freeway slabs, and banked concrete maze walls** to bypass blocked or crumbling ground lanes.

Vehicles run on **different power architectures**, fundamentally changing how you fuel, cool, and survive each biome:

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fuel (Combustion / High-Octane)** | Heavy mechanical roar, raw high-RPM power, huge synergy with **Cyclone Turbocharger** & muscle **Winch/Towbar** builds. | Smash red fuel drums, ruptured tankers, glowing sap pods, or crush fuel-bloated mosquitoes under tyres. | **Unlocks Exhaust Backfire Dump** (burns fuel for rear flames). **Risk:** Fuel-sucking mosquitoes actively target your fuel lines! |

### C. Functional Equipable Vehicle Parts (Modular Survival Hardware)

Every exterior part bolted onto the car serves a **real mechanical purpose** in the physics & hazard simulation:

1. **Functional Spoilers (Rear Aero — Equipable Types)**
   - **High-Downforce / Active DRS Wing:** Nails rear traction during slick Acid Rain corners and tilts flat on straights for peak exit velocity.
   - **Air-Brake Flap Spoiler:** Snaps upright under hard braking to shed speed violently before a sudden Sinkhole drop, closing concrete monolith, or snapped bridge edge.
   - **Mid-Air Glider / Trim Wing:** Deploys stabilizing aero flaps during huge jumps (boxcar ramps, sinkhole vaults, hydro-dam leaps) to control chassis pitch and stretch airtime across wide chasms.

2. **Functional Canards (Front Bumper Aero — Equipable Types)**
   - **High-Grip Dive-Plane Canards:** Sharpens front-end bite so the nose still turns crisply when Acid Rain slashes Pacejka tyre grip (`μ`).
   - **Debris-Slicer / Vine-Cutter Canards:** Reinforced razor-edged front winglets that **slice clean through hanging lab vines, anchor-grub tendrils, and spore stalks** before they snag your hood.
   - **Vortex-Deflector Canards:** Channel Acid Rain spray, mud, and dust outward away from the windshield while funneling cleaner airflow into the **Cyclone Turbine / Turbo Air Vents**.

3. **Deployable Solar Panel (Emergency Backup Power Rig)**
   - An equipable folding photovoltaic array mounted flush on the roof or rear deck.
   - **When Fuel / Battery Hits 0%:** Instead of being permanently dead, the player can **deploy the Solar Panel** — unfolding armored wings that trickle-charge emergency limp/restart power from overhead sunlight (super strategic inside the **Piston Labyrinth**, where you have to coast into a daylight sky-shaft between 20 m concrete walls to catch rays — while underground subways or heavy Acid Rain clouds slow charging and expose the open panels to debris!).

4. **Equipable Winch & Towbar (Built for Muscle & Heavy Power Cars)**
   - **Front Hydraulic Winch:** Fires a heavy braided steel cable onto concrete maze pillars, crane booms, girders, or rocky ledges — letting you **haul yourself back up a collapsing sinkhole lip, drag your chassis up a steep tilted ship deck or near-vertical ramp, rip down a fractured brick wall blocking a shortcut, or whip-pivot tight 90° maze corners**.
   - **Heavy Rear Towbar / Pull Hitch:** Built for high-torque muscle & power rigs — hook onto **wrecked junkers, fallen concrete slabs, or steel barricades to yank blocked doorways open**, or **tow explosive fuel drums / ruptured tankers straight into a giant carnivorous plant's mouth or reinforced concrete gate** before detaching!

---

### D. Universal Chassis Emergency Moves (Built Into Every Vehicle)
- **Power-Source Purge:** Every car can dump a small burst of its active power source to shed clinging threats — **Exhaust Backfire Flame** *(Fuel)*, **Thermal Steam/Rad Vent** *(Nuclear)*, **High-Pressure Steam Blast** *(Hydro)*, or **Static Hull Discharge** *(Electric)*.
- **Rotor-Cook Pulse (Throttle + Brake):** Spikes brake rotor heat red-hot to **cook Wheel-Well Leech Slugs straight out of the wheel arches** and flash-dry acid slickness off tyres.

## 7. Teleport Portal Meta-Mechanic: **Momentum Carry-Over & Blind Split Portals**

### A. Momentum, State & Hardware Carry-Over
Because there are no laps or reset grids, **every portal preserves your exact physical state**:
- Enter a portal at 160 km/h mid-air → burst out of the exit portal in the next biome still flying at 160 km/h (with your **Glider Spoiler** holding pitch straight through the transition!).
- Carry latched parasites, fire, acid slickness, fuel/battery/reactor-heat state, mounted Tool Pickups, and active Vehicle Abilities straight across worlds:
  - Drag Dead Forest weight-ticks through a portal straight into a Demolish Run C4 fireball or Wasteland Acid Rain squall to wipe your chassis clean in one second.
  - Suck up a lungful of falling skyscraper glass with your **Cyclone Vortex Turbine** in Sinkhole Downtown, blast through the exit portal into Overgrown Lab, and unleash the shrapnel hurricane straight into a lunging giant plant!
  - Let your **Hydro** car drink Acid Rain to 100% charge right before teleporting into a dry Nuclear Wasteland, or deploy your **Solar Panel** inside a Piston Labyrinth sunbeam shaft before hacking the final hydraulic exit gate open.

### B. Blind Split Portals (Choose Your Next Biome at 180 km/h)
- At the end of a gauntlet, **2 or 3 giant shimmering portals open side-by-side**, each showing a live visual slice of the chaos waiting inside (e.g., Acid Rain Piston Labyrinth vs. Orbital Rocket Launch Trench vs. Capsizing Mega-Ship).
- Players pick their next hell **at full throttle based on their current build, power source, and emergency state**:
  - Running low in a **Hydro** car? Dive into the Acid Rain portal to refill on the move!
  - Covered in heavy weight-ticks or alien vines? Dive straight into the **Orbital Launch Trench** to roast every latched creature off in seconds!
  - Rocking **Downforce Suction Fans** + **Acoustic Resonance Horn**? Pick **The Active Blast Quarry** to climb the 50° cliffs and shatter falling boulders!
